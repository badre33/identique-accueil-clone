The Link Agency website's primary objective is **lead generation**.

How to apply:
- All CTAs prioritize WhatsApp / contact form / qualified discovery call
- Case studies KPIs must emphasize lead-gen metrics (CPL, qualified leads/month, conversion rate, cost of acquisition, demands for quote) over vanity metrics (raw reach, vague engagement)
- Hero, services, blog endings should funnel toward contact
- Numbers must remain plausible for the Moroccan market (audiences 100K–3M, growth 30–90%, not 200%+)
